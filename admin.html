3. Get the titles and play counts

Suno doesn’t let other websites read its data, so this page can’t fetch it directly. Instead, a small script runs on suno.com (where you’re signed in) and hands you the list.

Computer: open suno.com, press F12, open Console, paste, press Enter (type “allow pasting” first if asked). A box appears with your songs; copy it and paste below.
Phone/tablet: save a new bookmark with any name, then edit it and replace its address with the copied text. Open suno.com, tap that bookmark, copy the box that appears, and paste below.
