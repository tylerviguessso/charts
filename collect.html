Suno Top 50 Collect

Turns a copied Suno playlist page into a clean list of titles and play counts. Your list is remembered in this browser until you clear it.

1. Paste a playlist page

Suno loads about 50 songs at a time. Scroll down to load more, copy again, and paste again: songs you add twice are merged, not duplicated, and the newest count wins.

2. Your list

Paste this into the box in step 3 of the admin page. Each line is Title | Artist | Plays.
